How this works

Geometry runs on a simple rhythm. Stephen teaches himself the lesson, then Colton comes Tuesday and Thursday to make it real — measure something, build something, and find out whether the maths actually closes.

  1. Work the practice here. Every lesson is one page: a reading, then two sets of practice with a hint and a worked answer on each question. No grade, no clock.
  2. Flag what didn’t land. Tick Still fuzzy on anything that doesn’t click. That list is the agenda for the session — it costs nothing to be honest about.
  3. Download your answers when you’re done, and download the file and drop it into the Inbox folder of your Obsidian vault. That’s your own archive, and Colton sees it.
  4. Bring your hands Tuesday. The session card tells you what to gather — tacks and string, a protractor, a tape measure, a clock face.

Nothing here needs an account, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. The page runs entirely on your device; downloading is the only way anything leaves it.
